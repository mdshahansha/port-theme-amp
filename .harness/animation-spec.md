## Scroll classification

**Homepage:** Marketing navigation is static in the measured sample: at scrollY 225 px, its top was −225 px. No homepage pinning was established; sampled visible positioned nodes inside the marketing root had no sticky/fixed positions. A mobile toggle outside that root remained available, but its complete positioning contract was not measured.

Mobile fast/reverse scrolling returned to the recorded 3798 px position, with ordinary section flow and continuous decoration. Mid-page reload retained scrollY 3825 px while the title restarted from the initial phrase. These samples do not prove absence of all parallax or reveals. Exact activation thresholds, normalized trajectories, reserved pin space and homepage back-navigation scroll restoration are **UNKNOWN**.

**Supporting Orb page:** Section navigation has sticky top 96 px and the media panel has sticky top 0 px. Frame-to-section scroll progress was not captured.

**Documentation:** Sidebar has sticky top 48 px, height equal to viewport minus 48 px, and its own overflow-y: auto.

Source classes support style observations; they do not identify a scroll library. No global smooth-scroll or scroll-snapping mechanism was confirmed. See [animation-timelines.md](animation-timelines.md), [responsive-spec.md](responsive-spec.md) and [unknowns.md](unknowns.md) for unresolved behavior.

## Declaration versus runtime boundary

HOME-CSSOM-001 confirms paths/alternatives; HOME-TIMED-DOM-001 additionally confirms sampled exit easing/delays/origin, DOM phase coexistence and approximate insertion cadence. Neither supplies first-frame video, exact full-cycle scheduler, runtime reduced-motion emulation, hover activation or complete focus traversal. CSS selectors cannot be reported as completed behavioral tests. No font/video/image asset was downloaded or exported.
