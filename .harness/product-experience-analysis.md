# Product experience analysis

Primary source: [live homepage](https://ampcode.com/), HOME-STRUCT-001. Interpretation below is **INFERRED from observed communication**, not a claim about private product internals.

Amp is presented as a coding agent and development environment for developers using remote and local agents. The current central proposition is continuous work across machines: a thread gets a remote orb, and users can leave their laptop or resume elsewhere. The rotating headline communicates coordination, output and continuity. The first-screen orange Start action leads to sign-up; navigation Sign In is outlined, while Docs, Models, Pricing and Chronicle give lower-commitment routes to learn and compare.

Recognizable identity comes from narrow editorial serif letterforms, extra-light italic emphasis, a compact geometric wordmark, dark teal/ivory/orange on the homepage, warm haze and grain, technical mono labels, dotted grid rails and asymmetric spatial alignment. Supporting marketing pages use cream, near-black and blue. Documentation deliberately uses a quieter near-white sans-serif reading environment. A single global theme applied indiscriminately would lose these distinctions.

| Section | Business purpose / user question | Observed visual/interaction strategy | Reconstruction significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Why change my way of working? | Rotating outcome phrases; surrounding real-looking task prompts; centered CTA and two small explanation popovers | Preserve reserved title geometry, italics, particle depth, current phrase semantics and explanations |
| Demo | Can this work on real tasks? | Long screen-plus-presenter walkthrough; six shortcuts and chapter/transcript navigation | Keep synchronized streams and useful navigation, rather than reproducing the private product depicted |
| Orbs bento | How do I preview, review, collaborate and automate? | Five linked HTML/SVG miniatures within a measured asymmetric grid | Layout and illustration anatomy matter; screenshots alone are an inadequate asset substitute |
| Testimonials | Are others getting a different experience? | Nine prominent attributed quotes and three continuous compact rows, with playful drifting Pucks | Preserve attribution, external destinations, loop direction and unobstructed readability |
| Subscription bridge | Can I use what I already pay for? | Two CTA choices and a short usage list over a blurred band | Distinguish public explanation from the authenticated linking action |
| Episodes | How do I begin and learn? | Four ordered lessons, durations and bounded horizontal strip | Preserve sequence, thumbnail ratio and scroll/control states |
| CLI | Does this fit my terminal workflow? | Platform-specific command selector, copy controls and local/remote demos | Correct command state, readable overflow and public docs links are essential |
| News/footer | Is the product active and trustworthy? Where next? | Dated updates, selected artwork, status/legal links and grouped directory | Dates/content are snapshot data; map destination intent accurately |

Historical leads in the brief—Everything Is Changing, Use Agents Anywhere, Ruthlessly On The Frontier, Only What We Love, Fast, Extensible—were not found as that exact homepage section sequence. Related frontier/opinionated themes remain in supporting product/about/docs text, but they must not become invented homepage sections. Primary current reconstruction scope is the live sequence in [page-specs/home.md](page-specs/home.md).
